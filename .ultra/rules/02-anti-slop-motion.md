# 02 — Anti-slop motion

- Every cut must land on a beat, a word, or a measured hold; no random float.
- Hooks resolve inside the style hook window; never open with a slow logo sting.
- Punch-ins stay inside the style range; combine camera moves, never stack competing zooms.
- Captions use active-word emphasis only; no full-sentence rainbow karaoke.
- Palette colors come from DESIGN.md/plan.json only. No stock gradients, no template icons.
- Grain, halation and halftone are texture, not haze: keep them under the style opacity caps.
- Run the lint: clipping, contrast, palette, overlap and social safe-zone defects are release blockers.
