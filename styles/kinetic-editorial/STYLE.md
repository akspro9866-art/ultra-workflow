# Kinetic Editorial — 16:9 explainer grammar

A documentary explainer system in the Vox tradition: argument first, texture second. Warm paper, charcoal type, one vermilion accent. Chapters are written, not decorated — every card makes a claim before it shows a graphic.

## Pacing

- Hook ASL **1.5–2.5s**, body ASL **2.5–4.5s**, outro **3–5s**. Let a viewer finish a thought; never let a frame sit past **4.5s**.
- Silence trim at **220ms**, J-cuts **240ms**, L-cuts **300ms**. Audio edits are softer than the shortform system.
- Beat-snap target **45%** — cut for argument structure, not for every kick drum.

## Type & captions

Ultra Display carries chapter titles and pull quotes. Ultra Sans handles captions in **3–6 word** chunks, title case, with a **vermilion underline** on the active word. The caption band sits at **72–86% height**, inside the 16:9 six-percent title-safe perimeter.

## Motion

Slow, confident moves: `easeInOutSine` dominant, punch-ins capped at **1.08**, two motion-blur samples for subtle camera blur. SVG callout arrows draw with a dash offset before the stat lands. Chapter cards rule in from the left margin.

## Color & texture

Paper `#F6F0E6`, aged surface `#E8DFCE`, ink `#1C1B18`, muted pencil `#6A645A`, vermilion `#E4572E`, deep teal `#2F5D62`. Grade adds a warm +0.12 temperature and a light 0.12 vignette. Grain **0.06**, halftone **0.10** at micro scale — printed matter, not a filter.

## Sound

**−14 LUFS**, **80–110 BPM** understated bed. Chapter → chime, data callout → pop, transitions → soft whoosh, UI accents → click. Duck music **14 dB** under narration.

## Non-negotiables

No neon startup gradients. No caption walls. No whip pans. No stock imagery of handshakes or boardrooms. If a graphic does not advance the argument, cut it.
