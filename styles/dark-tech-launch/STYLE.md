# Dark Tech Launch — 16:9 product-launch grammar

An obsidian launch system for SaaS and hardware reveals. It borrows the restraint of Apple keynotes and the precision of Linear's product films: one claim, one product frame, one luminous accent. Motion is 2.5D and deliberate; nothing orbits, nothing bounces.

## Pacing

Hook **1.5–2.5s**, body **2.5–4.5s**, outro **2.5–4s**, static hold ceiling **4s**. Trim silence at **200ms**; keep short **80ms J-cuts** and **160ms L-cuts** for tight narration. Half the cuts should land on a beat.

## Type & captions

Ultra Display for product claims, set in sentence case at generous size. Ultra Sans for captions in **3–6 word** chunks with the active word shifted to mint. Mono labels carry specs and timestamps. Captions sit at **70–84% height**, inside the 16:9 title-safe box.

## Motion

`easeInOutCubic` glides with a **1.00 → 1.10** punch-in ceiling. Two motion-blur samples keep camera moves cinematic. Feature badges float on a 2.5D plane with 12px parallax, and glow pulses sync to the beat — not to wall-clock time.

## Color & texture

Obsidian `#07090D`, raised surface `#0E131C`, ice white `#F2F6FF`, steel `#8FA0B8`, mint `#7CF5C7`, electric blue `#4F7CFF`. Grade +0.08 exposure, +16% contrast, a −0.08 cooling shift and a **0.30 vignette**. Grain 0.03, halation 0.18 — specular, not smoky.

## Sound

**−14 LUFS**, **90–124 BPM**. Hero → riser, reveal → whoosh, accent → chime, stat → pop. Duck music **12 dB** under voice.

## Non-negotiables

No rainbow gradients. No orbiting logos. No HUD clutter. No unlabeled benchmark charts. If a UI frame appears, it must show a real interface state with readable labels.
