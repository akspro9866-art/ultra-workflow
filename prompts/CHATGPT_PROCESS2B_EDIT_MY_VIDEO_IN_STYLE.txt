2. Upload my_video.mp4; run `node ultra/ultra.mjs raw-probe my_video.mp4 --style <slug> --out workspace/raw-probe`. Inspect raw-edit-plan.json: silences/dead air, keepSegments, hookEnd, targetBodyAslSec, maxStaticHoldSec, punchInPoints, brollCutaways, captionWindows.

6. `render`; deliver build/ultra-final.mp4 + compliance block + match score + patch history.
