8. Rule 5 STYLE VERIFY: `$ULTRA verify --style <slug> --draft build/draft.mp4`. Requirements: quantitative pass (stutterEvents === 0, no black frames, LUFS within ±1.5 of the style target), style compliance all OK (ASL ranges, maxStaticHoldSec, banned transitions, palette distance), and a generated build/audit-frames/style-match.png. Inspect it natively and grade Style Match (0–100) against STYLE EXEMPLAR vs YOUR EDIT. Patch weak scenes via `$ULTRA patch` or `$ULTRA ralph auto-fix` and repeat build+verify (max 3 rounds) until score ≥ 90.
9. SUBMIT: `$ULTRA render --4k` → `$ULTRA export-project` → `$ULTRA guard --stage delivery`. Deliver build/ultra-final.mp4 + editable-project.zip + style compliance block + match score + patch history.
